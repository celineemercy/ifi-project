# Content Sources and Status

## Content status

The learning material in this repository is **original prototype content**. It is not an official IFI curriculum, policy manual, operating procedure, or employee evaluation framework.

Public sources were used only to ground the prototype in authentic institutional context:

- IFI operates across cultural, linguistic, university, and scientific cooperation.
- IFI has public-facing activity in courses, cultural events, Campus France, and médiathèques.
- Institut français emphasizes intercultural dialogue, cultural mediation, public development, professional learning, and multilingualism.

Service behaviors, lesson wording, quiz answers, simulated visitor conversations, and scoring guidance were written specifically for this university prototype.

Member courses are also original prototype content. They use introductory French expressions and cultural-exchange themes; they do not reproduce IFI course materials or qualify learners for an IFI certificate.

Member package names, inclusions, and IDR prices are invented for the demo. They are not sourced from or endorsed by IFI and must not be presented as real offers.

## Editorial safeguards

- Do not add operational claims unless an IFI stakeholder verifies them.
- Do not describe prototype scenarios as records of real incidents.
- Do not use names of real IFI employees in seeded data.
- Label scores as learning feedback, never formal performance evaluation.
- When stakeholder-provided workshop material becomes available, record its provenance and replace prototype lessons through a reviewed content update.
